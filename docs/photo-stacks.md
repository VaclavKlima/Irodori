# Photo stacks and capture sequences

Stacks are flat, persistent groups in the managed library. Each photograph belongs to at most one stack. A new stack starts collapsed; its cover remains an ordinary selectable and editable photograph. The separate expand button reports the member count. Derived photographs also report their immutable recipe source count, which can differ from the stack's membership.

Expanded members appear immediately below the cover with a grouping marker. The cover stays first, followed by older results and originals ordered by precise capture time, with import time as each missing date's fallback. A stack can cross a library page; the first member on the following page identifies its cover. Pages contain 60 visible photo rows. The library heading reports stored photographs, while page controls and Previous/Next follow visible rows.

Use **Actions** or a photo's context menu:

- **Group selected photos** accepts two or more ungrouped photographs of any supported format. The active selected photograph becomes the initial cover.
- **Select group (N photos)** explicitly selects every member, including members hidden by collapse or pagination. It replaces the selected set while preserving the active photograph and range anchor.
- **Set this photo as group cover**, **Remove this photo from group**, and **Ungroup (N photos)** organize photographs without changing originals, processing identities, edits, or merge recipes.
- **Delete group (N photos)…** opens the existing Trash confirmation for every current member, including derived results. It requires no preceding group selection.
- **Merge K group originals to HDR…** and **Stack K group originals for noise reduction…** open the existing merge review using only current originals (`assetKind !== 'derived'`) in persisted order. Previous results remain in the group and are excluded from inputs. Immutable recipe source lists do not determine these targets.
- **Group capture sequences…** starts the metadata scan described below.

The menu separates **Photo**, **Selected photos (N)**, and **Group (N photos)** sections. The toolbar uses the active photograph's group; right-clicking a selected photograph retains selection, while right-clicking an unselected photograph selects it alone. Photo cover/removal actions explicitly name their scope, and **Delete this photo…** appears when its target differs from selection. The group section reports originals separately from the full membership. Group merge is disabled below two or above 32 originals, or for known unsupported formats, with an accessible explanation; originals are never silently omitted. Review validation remains authoritative for verified Sony cameras, numeric capture metadata, compatibility, and exposure limits.

Expansion, collapse, and group actions other than **Select group** preserve the active preview, selected IDs, and range anchor until an operation changes the library. The gallery names selected photographs hidden in collapsed stacks. A hidden active photograph or range anchor uses its cover's visible position for navigation and range boundaries. Clicking a collapsed cover selects only that cover. Delete/Backspace and the selection section's Delete action target the selected managed bundles, even when a group item is focused or a menu action is awaiting resolution.

The complete revision-checked snapshot loads before group counts appear, and group actions revalidate that displayed revision. A changed or unavailable group closes the menu and reports the problem without acting. Dismissed, superseded, or cancelled requests cannot reopen menus or confirmations; repeated activation is guarded. Menu height stays within the viewport, scrolling is available, and keyboard focus brings items into view. Initial focus goes to selection deletion; arrows, Home/End, Escape, outside-click dismissal, and origin focus restoration remain supported.

**Shift-click** replaces selection with the inclusive gallery range. Every cover at either boundary or inside the range contributes all current stack members, even when the stack is collapsed or spans pages. The same rules apply in either direction, including a cover-to-same-cover range. **Ctrl/Cmd+Shift-click** adds the resolved range. A range between expanded members includes only the members within its boundaries when their cover is outside the range. **Click** and **Ctrl/Cmd-click** select or toggle one photograph; individual members can be deselected after selecting a whole stack. Shift-click without an anchor behaves as an ordinary click. The clicked photograph remains active; range selection retains its anchor.

Selection actions finish in arrival order, independently of gallery refreshes and page requests. Paging and background import preserve pending selection. Deletion waits for all preceding selection work and cannot proceed after a failed resolution until a later selection succeeds. Deleted IDs are pruned from delayed responses. The confirmation freezes the resolved IDs and shows their full count and every filename in a bounded, keyboard-scrollable list; subsequent grouping changes cannot add deletion targets. Cancel receives initial focus, Escape closes the confirmation, and focus returns to the originating control.

Group deletion also waits for pending selection, resolves the complete group, then freezes its references for the same filename disclosure and `deletePhotos(ids)` flow. Group merge freezes original IDs for the existing review and source leases. **Select group** resolves its revision-checked snapshot inside the selection queue and prunes IDs deleted during resolution. Accepted background imports allow deletion and merge through the foreground scheduler; organization keeps its existing task restrictions. System Trash, cancellation, stack repair, and removal recovery are shared with selection deletion.

Successful merge publication unions the complete memberships of its sources' stacks, retains unused originals and older results, makes the new result the cover, and collapses the stack. Catalog version 11 backfills surviving stored merge relationships once. Overlapping relationships form a single stack with the newest result as cover; subsequent restarts respect manual changes.

Successful deletion and interrupted-removal recovery repair memberships inside the catalog transaction. Removing a cover promotes the newest surviving result, otherwise the first ordered member. Fewer than two surviving members dissolve the stack. Failed Trash calls leave memberships intact, and cancellation waits for the current OS move. Recoverable and OS-restored bundles retain the existing removal-journal behavior.

## Verified automatic grouping

The enabled profiles cover the observed **Sony ZV-1A ARW** recording modes. Private originals and hashes live in an ignored fixture manifest; redistribution is not authorized. The original twelve-frame continuous capture (`DSC03246`–`DSC03257`) and nine separate shutter presses (`DSC03258`–`DSC03266`) remain part of the evidence.

| Capture         | ReleaseMode / ReleaseMode2 / ReleaseMode3 / SequenceLength | Timing                                                                   |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Continuous      | `2 / 1 / 1 / 0`                                            | Adjacent gap at most one second                                          |
| Continuous high | `2 / 33 / 1 / 0`                                           | Adjacent gap at most one second                                          |
| Continuous BRK  | `5 / 2 / 2 / 3`                                            | Adjacent gap at most `max(previous shutter, current shutter) + 1 second` |

Every member must agree on camera identity, including available body serial, format and recorded timezone offset. All three frame counters must agree. A sequence starts at frame 1, has at least two contiguous frames and strictly increasing precise timestamps. The observed modes have no `DriveMode` tag. Counter resets and mode changes delimit captures. Continuous BRK frames 1 and 2 group immediately as an incomplete three-frame capture; frame 3 extends the same group. A lone frame, absent start, interior gap, contradictory counters or ambiguous timestamps stays available for manual grouping. Single-frame BRK (`5 / 23 / 2 / 1`) records separate shutter presses and remains ungrouped.

Capture metadata **v2** adds optional numeric `shutterSeconds` to make long-exposure timing explicit. It retains original date/time, all fractional digits (including `006`), offsets, release fields and counters. The bundled worker reads ExifTool raw JSON without opening a RAW decoder or rebuilding previews. Missing offsets remain unknown. Cached v1 continuous records and their existing group keys remain usable. New imports write v2; **Group capture sequences…** refreshes older managed originals explicitly, without a catalog migration. Unknown timing for a non-single capture blocks detection for that camera because an overlap cannot be ruled out. Candidate fields are documented in [ExifTool's Sony reference](https://exiftool.sourceforge.net/TagNames/Sony.html); the reference alone does not verify trigger boundaries.

Successful imports reconcile committed capture facts across shuffled batches. Untouched automatic groups can extend or combine only when every existing member belongs to one newly verified capture. The surviving group contains the earliest already grouped frame and retains its cover and expansion. Membership changes commit in one transaction. Manual and merge groups, touched automatic groups, membership removals, cover changes and ungrouping overrides remain protected.

**Group capture sequences…** has fixed totals, per-photo errors and cancellation. Cancellation can retain completed metadata inspection but publishes no partial grouping. A later scan reuses current metadata. Group membership itself is never evidence for HDR compatibility: the same trigger verifier checks fresh managed-original facts frozen under merge source leases. Grouping refresh and review creation serialize inspections and worker cleanup, so one operation cannot close another's active capture inspection. Cancellation and shutdown drain admitted inspections while retaining source leases until review creation or disposal finishes. No capture grouping starts a merge automatically. Other cameras and recording modes remain unverified.

## Storage and API

Catalog version 11 separates `stacks`, `stack_members`, `capture_metadata`, `stack_overrides`, and the gallery revision from photo edits and `derived_assets`. Member IDs are unique. Cover, ordering, origin, expansion, and stack revision survive restarts. Flat `listPhotos`, navigation, and range enumeration remain available alongside the stack-aware gallery projection.

Preload and the editing MCP server share the main-process service. Stack operations take IDs and expected revisions. Manual grouping uses the gallery revision returned by `listStacks`; cover, expansion, removal, and ungrouping use the summary's stack revision. Members remain paginated. MCP exposes `luma_list_stacks`, `luma_get_photo_stack`, `luma_get_stack_members`, `luma_get_stack_snapshot`, `luma_group_photos`, `luma_ungroup_stack`, `luma_remove_from_stack`, `luma_set_stack_cover`, `luma_set_stack_expanded`, `luma_list_gallery`, `luma_locate_gallery_photo`, `luma_get_gallery_range`, and `luma_group_capture_sequences`. No renderer filesystem access is added.

`getStackSnapshot(stackId, expectedRevision): Promise<StackSnapshot>` and MCP `luma_get_stack_snapshot` return `{ stack: StackSummary, photos: Photo[] }`. Main resolves every current member synchronously in persisted order, rejects missing stacks and stale or invalid revisions, and refuses incomplete memberships. Counts cover collapsed and paginated photographs. This adds no database migration and retains paginated member and range APIs.

`getGalleryRange` and MCP `luma_get_gallery_range` resolve synchronously from one complete catalog view. Included covers expand from persisted memberships, independently of immutable merge recipe source counts. References are deduplicated in gallery order, with each included stack following its persisted member order. Renderer pagination and per-member IPC calls do not participate. Flat catalog range enumeration remains separate. This selection change requires no database migration.
