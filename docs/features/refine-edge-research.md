## Decision — November 13, 2026

The bounded study for [#77](https://github.com/VaclavKlima/Irodori/issues/77)
**rejects both candidates for broad implementation**. Keep
[#78](https://github.com/VaclavKlima/Irodori/issues/78) blocked. The user selected
automatic-first interaction and broad usefulness across the existing corpus;
class-limited improvements do not satisfy that choice. No Refine Edge control,
production model, settings migration or public API was added.

RGB guided reconstruction improves some dog-coat holes, wig curls and pine tips,
but it retains background-filled fence gaps and introduces uneven coverage of
thin flamingo legs. On the confirmation robin it spreads unwanted fractional
coverage over the explicitly excluded mealworm. Native overlay and masked-edit
inspection, including an independent second review, reject broad adoption.

CascadePSP failed its single unchanged FP32 ONNX export on adaptive average
pooling before native WebGPU inference. This is an export-route rejection, not a
photographic-quality failure. No alternative shape, opset, model, architecture
rewrite or CPU inference fallback followed. Its pretrained-weight adoption rights
also remain unresolved independently of the software's MIT license.

## Frozen method and reproducibility

The [protocol](../../assets/tests/references/refine-edge/protocol.json) and
[native crop selection](../../assets/tests/references/refine-edge/crops.json)
were frozen before candidate execution. All twelve original photographs are
tracked and match the existing corpus SHA-256 values. Source preparation used the
application's sensor RAW/raster worker, once-oriented native dimensions, neutral
As Shot SDR, and no crop, lens or local edits. The study opened no managed library.

Fresh SAM 2.1 Tiny baselines use the original box/positive/negative prompts and
pinned production model. Every candidate receives the same native binary baseline.
The comparator is the existing production Feather 4 / Expand-Contract 0 kernel.
Guided filtering reuses the existing RGB GPU implementation: 1024 grid, radius 8,
epsilon 0.001, matching RGB/coverage sampling, and native fractional R8 output.
Proof generation does not threshold the result back to binary.

Nine development scenes preceded a recorded rejection decision and explicit
release of three confirmation scenes. These former holdouts already had public
findings; they are **previously seen confirmation data**, not unseen validation.
No candidate parameters changed after their release. Initial runner invocations
were authorized after the external freeze receipt; retrospective manifest links
are explicitly labeled. Later invocations verify that receipt automatically and
retain per-invocation hashes. The original manifests and logs remain preserved.

The [tools and exact reproduction procedure](../../scripts/research/refine-edge/README.md)
retain source/model pins and output contracts. Research downloads, native masks,
proofs and measurements are ignored artifacts, not bundled resources.

## Available-device evidence

On Bazzite Linux x64, native Dawn reports RX 7900 XTX / RADV Mesa 26.2.6. Separate
ORT-only receipts identify PCI `0000:03:00.0`, with 465 encoder and 329 decoder
nodes assigned exclusively to `WebGpuExecutionProvider`, CPU fallback disabled.
The full twelve-scene baseline/guided/Feather comparison runs on that adapter.

The integrated Radeon Raphael representative uses the exact same 5496 × 3672
dog source and baseline. Its 20,181,312-byte guided and Feather outputs are each
byte-identical to the discrete adapter. Separate ORT evidence identifies PCI
`0000:18:00.0` with the same strict provider assignment. This is one native-source
comparison, not a repeat of the photographic corpus or general adapter parity.

The discrete guided call took 88.8 ms initially and 93.0/89.8/74.3 ms in three
subsequent calls; integrated results were 214.1 ms and 203.9/193.4/171.5 ms.
These include kernel setup and readback, with production pipelines created per
call. They exclude source decode, SAM, and proof construction. Gameplay was
concurrent; these are contextual measurements, not idle performance certification.

Automatic budgets were not overridden. The production PCI-capacity matcher could
not prove the descriptive Dawn identity, so admission correctly retained the
conservative 2 GiB GPU budget. Allocation estimates are not measured opaque
driver-memory peaks. Startup cancellation closed the owned worker and published
neither output nor temporary PNG; this does not qualify mid-inference draining.
Oversized dimensions were refused before allocation. Independent bounded GPU
tests separately cover buffer-limit refusal, destruction and same-device retry.

The actual dog baseline-to-guided base replacement passes seven model-free
reference checks with the existing two Paint/Erase strokes: retained IDs/order/
content, immutable bases, exact save/reload and Undo/Redo renders, and changed-grid
refusal. These are research representation checks, not application persistence,
crop/lens registration, HDR export or interaction acceptance.

## Stop and resumption

Stop this study with rejection. Do not enable a class-limited tool, tune another
guided-filter grid, repeat parameter sweeps or substitute a learned model.
Revisit #78 only after a separately selected candidate/proposal provides a clear
common-backend route, usable weight terms and evidence that can satisfy the broad
quality gate, including the retained thin-leg, mealworm and fence failures.
