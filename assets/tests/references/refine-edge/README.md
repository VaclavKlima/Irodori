# Refine Edge study references

These project-authored JSON research specifications follow the repository's
Apache-2.0 license. They contain no new photographic inputs or model weights.

- `protocol.json`: frozen automatic-first, broad-improvement decision gates and
  pinned existing corpus/reference hashes. Its SHA-256 is checked by the study
  gate tests; preserve its bytes after execution.
- `crops.json`: source-only native pixel regions frozen before candidate review.
  Existing former holdouts are disclosed as previously seen confirmation scenes.
- `cascade-sources.json`: upstream source/checkpoint pins, integrity and licensing
  evidence. Software and pretrained-weight rights are recorded separately.
- `pooling-followup.json`: separate bounded exact-pooling continuation; preserves
  the earlier frozen references and unchanged model/numerical/resource gates.
- `pooling-reference.json`: 147 original Torch outputs from a project-authored
  procedural input, with package, generator, binary-hash and numerical provenance.
- `metadata-followup.json`: separately authorized same-live-session assignment
  audit, bounded CPU shape bookkeeping and unchanged model/numerical gates.
- `metadata-photos.json`: conditional hard-photo preparation/review contract,
  explicitly selected native preparation and the stop after a decisive failure.

All twelve photographic originals and their provenance remain in their existing
paths under `assets/tests/images/` and the legacy `tests/fixtures/` tree. The
independent OpenCV numerical reference is reused from
`../automatic-selection/guided-filter.json` with its original generator and terms.
Downloads, source checkouts, raw coverage and proof images stay outside Git.

and [reproduction tools](../../../../scripts/research/refine-edge/README.md).
