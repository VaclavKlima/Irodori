On 2026-11-08 every entire original and every available embedded thumbnail or preview was visually reviewed. A second agent independently inspected the complete originals and source-license pages. Read-only ExifTool review included interpreted and unknown EXIF/maker/XMP/IPTC tags and embedded metadata. No identifying person, private document or screen, address, vehicle plate, GPS or private owner/contact metadata, or device serial was found in these selected files. The manifest records harmless software/profile creator values and the empty glass-mug OwnerName field. The bicycle contains a tiny defocused partial figure clipped at the far-left edge without an identifiable face. The wig has public historical museum description/accession text; the flamingos have bird leg rings. Neither is a private document or a device identifier.

The wig is a historical display object and does not supply human-portrait hair acceptance. The flamingo source is 1558 × 1148, so it cannot establish 20 MP feather quality; it contains no distinct mirror reflection. The robin is clipped by its source frame. Glass transmission does not provide controlled alpha ground truth. Predicted segmentation confidence is not measured alpha, and these photographs do not themselves supply manually annotated ground truth.

Generated previews, masks, measurements and overlays belong under ignored artifacts. Preserve these originals byte for byte and retain their photographer credits and license links when redistributing them or adapting image material.
