# Preset scenario icons

`scenarios.svg` contains original High noise and Soft look symbols drawn for
Irodori. The symbols use a 24 × 24 view box, rounded outline strokes and
`currentColor`, matching the application's Lucide icons. Render them at 16 or
24 pixels. They describe preset intent and do not add processing operations.

These original resources are licensed under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
Other scenario glyphs use the existing `lucide-react` dependency and its license.
The adjacent manifest records the sprite hash and provenance. Generated contact
sheets and screenshots remain in ignored verification artifacts.
